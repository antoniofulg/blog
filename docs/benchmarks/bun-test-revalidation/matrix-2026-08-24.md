# T8 finalist matrix

The four finalist pairs were run on Bun 1.4.0 with one discarded warm-up and
five valid interleaved samples per arm. Each arm executed the same 113 product
files (the 20 runner/benchmark infrastructure files remain explicit Vitest
dispositions). A sample was retained but excluded when the contamination sensor
observed another test workload; excluded attempts never contribute to medians.

## Outcome and skip parity

Every valid sample reported 113 files, 2,057 passed tests, 0 failed, 84
skipped, and 0 todo. The three fully skipped product files were retained in
both commands and normalized as skipped files using their manifest/leaf
identities:

- `auth-integ.test.ts`
- `indexer-integ.test.ts`
- `sync-integ.test.ts`

The local PostgreSQL probe returned unavailable on this host, so those files
were intentionally skipped in both runners. No `ECONNREFUSED` result is used as
benchmark evidence, and the benchmark subprocesses use a sanitized loopback
database URL rather than inherited external credentials.

## Derived metrics

Percentages use the Vitest arm as the baseline. RSS·time is the median peak RSS
in MiB multiplied by median wall time in seconds; it is an occupancy
approximation, not an integral of sampled RSS.

| Profile | Bun wall-time change | Bun serialized throughput | Bun peak RSS change | Bun RSS·time change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| isolated-1 | 31.10% faster | 45.15% higher | 21.90% higher | 16.02% lower |
| isolated-2 | 21.64% faster | 27.61% higher | 3.24% higher | 19.10% lower |
| isolated-4 | 4.33% slower | 4.15% lower | 10.71% lower | 6.84% lower |
| `--smol`-2 | 13.81% faster | 16.02% higher | 3.68% higher | 10.64% lower |

The matrix does not establish a universal Bun winner: Bun wins elapsed time at
1/2 workers and `--smol`-2, while Vitest wins elapsed time at 4 workers. Bun's
4-worker profile has the lower instantaneous peak RSS; the other profiles have
slightly or substantially higher Bun peak RSS. This is why the final
recommendation is profile-scoped and does not silently change the default or
CI route.
